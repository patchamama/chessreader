# Commentary quality, book layout, Lichess access and offline games
Objective: make the automatic commentary say what annotators say (and stop saying wrong things), lay it out like a chess book (paragraphs, diagrams), fix Lichess access, and close the open limits of study-tools. Branch: main (user works on main; the demo deploys from main).
Delivery: one commit per task, pushed to main after its checks. Runner: Vitest (apps/web, packages/chess-shared), PHPUnit (apps/api), tsc, ESLint; measurement with packages/chess-shared/scripts/evaluate-commentary.mjs; browser smoke on the demo.

- L5 T3: reviewGame now looks ahead (lookAhead): every move that emptied a square between king and rook before a castling says so with the move it comes on; up to 3 earlier moves whose piece started to aim at the square of a mate or of a strong blow by the enemy king say "apunta a f7, junto al rey: allí se decide la partida en la jugada N"; a capture that keeps a jump of ≥20 points in winning chances and is followed within 6 plies by ≥2 points of material or a mate "starts a combination". moveMotifs: joint attack names the pieces it joins ("se suma al ataque del alfil de b2 sobre el peón de e5"), pawns count when the attack on them was already under way; "overloaded" when the moved piece is the only defender of ≥2 attacked pieces; "trapped" when an enemy piece becomes threatened and none of its moves reaches safety; mate lists the king's squares and what covers them (king removed so squares behind it count). The evaluator now runs reviewGame (as the product) instead of moveMotifs alone.
